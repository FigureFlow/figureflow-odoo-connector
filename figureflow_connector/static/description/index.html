FigureFlow — AI CFO

Connect Odoo to FigureFlow's AI agents for reconciliation, month-end close, KPIs and cash forecasting.

What you get

  • Automated reconciliation — AI matches bank statements against your Odoo journal entries and proposes adjustments.
  • Month-end close — Guided workflow with cut-off checks, accrual recommendations, opening-balance validation and approval gates.
  • 40+ KPIs and threshold alerts — Liquidity, profitability, solvency and efficiency metrics with daily/weekly digests.
  • Cash forecasting — Driver-based projections with scenario modelling and WebSocket-powered real-time updates.
  • Management and annual reports — AI-generated narratives, jurisdiction-aware compliance (SE, UK, CA).

How it works

  1. Install this module.
  2. Go to Settings → FigureFlow and click Connect to FigureFlow.
  3. This module generates a personal Odoo API key, securely hands it to figureflow.com over HTTPS, and opens FigureFlow in a new tab.
  4. Sign up or log in on FigureFlow to complete the connection.
  5. FigureFlow syncs your chart of accounts, journal entries, partners and invoices, then activates the AI features.

Data you share with FigureFlow

After you click Connect, this module shares your Odoo URL, database name, login and a freshly-generated personal API key with figureflow.com. FigureFlow then uses those credentials to read:

  • Chart of accounts (account.account)
  • Journal entries and lines (account.move, account.move.line)
  • Partners (res.partner)
  • Invoices and credit notes
  • Journals and tax definitions

Credentials never travel through your browser; the handshake is server-to-server over HTTPS. You can revoke access at any time by deleting the API key from your Odoo user preferences or by clicking Disconnect in the settings page. See the FigureFlow privacy policy for full details.
