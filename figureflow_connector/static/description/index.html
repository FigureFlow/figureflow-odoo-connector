Month-End Close

A close workflow that actually closes

Most close software is a checklist. Ours runs the close. Procedures execute in dependency order, quality gates block bad data from passing, approval gates wait for the human, and the whole thing learns across sessions.

Opening-balance validation

Reconciles prior period close → current period open. Detects roll-forward issues before they cascade.

Cut-off & unrecorded checks

Late vendor invoices, customer payments after period end, accruals that should have been booked.

VAT reconciliation

Output VAT vs input VAT vs balance sheet positions. Variance explanations.

Quality gates

Cumulative balance checks, TB sign-flip detection, unknown account codes, partial-result salvage.

Approval gates

Adjustments wait for human sign-off. Auto-approve toggle for routine cases. Full audit trail.

Cross-session learning

What you fixed last month informs how the close runs this month. The system gets sharper over time.

  • Chart of accounts (account.account)
  • Journal entries and lines (account.move, account.move.line)

Security promises

  • Nothing is sent before you explicitly click Connect
  • Credentials are handed server-to-server over HTTPS — they never travel through your browser
  • Write-back (drafts, posts, edits) is always two-stage: preview → your approval → execute. Nothing posts automatically
  • Multi-tenant isolation enforced at every layer — your data is never visible to another customer
  • Disconnect revokes the Odoo API key and triggers a purge of your data from FigureFlow
