Month-End Close

A close workflow that actually closes

Most close software is a checklist. Ours runs the close. Procedures execute in dependency order, quality gates block bad data, approval gates wait for the human, and the system learns across sessions.

Opening-balance validation

Reconciles prior period close → current period open. Detects roll-forward issues before they cascade.

Cut-off & unrecorded checks

Late vendor invoices, customer payments after period end, accruals that should have been booked.

VAT reconciliation

Output VAT vs input VAT vs balance sheet positions. Variance explanations.

Quality gates

Cumulative balance checks, TB sign-flip detection, unknown account codes, partial-result salvage.

Approval gates

Adjustments wait for human sign-off. Auto-approve toggle for routine cases. Full audit trail.

Cross-session learning

What you fixed last month informs how the close runs this month. The system sharpens over time.

Your data. Your terms.

Nothing leaves Odoo without your click. Nothing writes back without your approval. Disconnect anytime.

What FigureFlow reads from your Odoo

  • Chart of accounts (account.account)
  • Journal entries and lines (account.move, account.move.line)
  • Partners — customers and vendors (res.partner)
  • Invoices, credit notes, payments
  • Journals and tax definitions
  • Company info, currency, country — used to configure your jurisdiction

Security promises

  • Nothing is read from Odoo before you connect in FigureFlow
  • You create the Odoo API key yourself and provide it to FigureFlow over HTTPS — FigureFlow reads your data through Odoo's standard External API
  • Write-back (drafts, posts, edits) is always two-stage: preview → your approval → execute. Nothing posts automatically.
  • Multi-tenant isolation enforced at every layer — your data is never visible to another customer
  • Revoke access anytime by deleting the API key in your Odoo profile; disconnecting in FigureFlow purges your data
  • Full privacy policy at figureflow.app/privacy
